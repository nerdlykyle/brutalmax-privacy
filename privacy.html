BrutalMaxer Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Applies to BrutalMaxer 4.0 and later on iPhone and Android. Developer: Kyle Parsons (Motion Potion) · Contact: Kyle@motionpotion.studio

The short version

  • No account. No sign-up. No ads. No tracking.
  • Your workouts, programs, bodyweight and settings stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The only thing that leaves your phone is your purchase record, so Pro can unlock and restore.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into BrutalMaxer is saved in the app's own storage on your device:

  • Workouts, sets, reps, weights, notes and how each set felt
  • Programs you build, copy, edit or import, and any muscle focus you choose
  • Bodyweight entries and your goal
  • Settings, and the daily backups the app makes

Backups are files on your device. On iPhone they're in the Files app under On My iPhone › BrutalMaxer › Backups. On Android they're in the app's private storage. If you share a backup or a program file, you choose where it goes; we never see it.

What leaves your phone

Purchases (RevenueCat). When you buy or restore Pro, the App Store or Google Play handles the payment. We use RevenueCat to check that the purchase is valid. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history (product, price, date, store) and basic device details such as country and app version. We don't receive your payment details, name or email. This data is used only to unlock Pro and to understand sales. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Fonts. The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address when they load. See Google's privacy policy.

Custom exercise pictures. Every built-in exercise uses a 3D demo built into the app. If you add a custom exercise that has no 3D match, its picture may load from ExerciseDB's image server, which can see your IP address.

All of this travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Apple Health and Health Connect (optional, Pro)

If you turn on sync in Settings and allow access:

  • Workouts: finished workouts are written to Apple Health or Health Connect as strength training sessions (type, start and end time).
  • Bodyweight: weights you log are written there, and weights from your scale or other apps are read in, so your Progress chart stays complete. Edits and deletions you make in BrutalMaxer are applied to the same entries.

BrutalMaxer reads only bodyweight and writes only workouts and bodyweight. Health data stays on your device, between BrutalMaxer and Apple Health or Health Connect. It is never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising, and never used to decide eligibility for credit, insurance or employment. You can turn sync off at any time in BrutalMaxer, and remove access in Apple Health or Health Connect settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share your data.
  • We don't use analytics, ad networks or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, location or photos.

Delete your data

To delete your workout data: in BrutalMaxer go to Settings › Backups and delete any backups, then uninstall the app (or on Android, Settings › Apps › BrutalMaxer › Storage › Clear storage). Data you synced to Apple Health or Health Connect stays there; delete it in that app.

To delete your purchase record: email Kyle@motionpotion.studio with the subject "Delete my BrutalMaxer data" and the email on your App Store or Google Play receipt. We delete your RevenueCat record (the anonymous app user ID and purchase history) within 30 days. Apple and Google keep their own receipts under their policies, so you can still restore Pro with your store account.

Children

BrutalMaxer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
