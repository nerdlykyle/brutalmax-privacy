# brutalmax-privacy
Privacy policy for BrutalMax: Workout Tracker

Last Updated: April 2026

BrutalMaxer ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the BrutalMaxer mobile application.

1. Information We Collect
To provide our core tracking features, we collect the following:
* Account Data: If you create an account, we securely store your email address and authentication details via Firebase.
* Fitness & Health Data: We collect the workout data you input, including exercises, sets, reps, and weights. 
* Usage Data: We may collect anonymized, standard crash logs and app usage metrics to ensure the app functions correctly.

2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is strictly used to:
* Operate the workout tracker and warm-up calculator.
* Sync your workout history across your devices.
* Facilitate App Store / Google Play In-App Purchases.

3. Data Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or fitness data to third parties. Data is only shared with essential service providers (such as Google/Firebase for database hosting and Apple/Google for processing secure payments) strictly for the purpose of operating the app.

4. Data Retention and Deletion
You own your data. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated fitness data at any time by contacting us or using the "Delete Account" feature within the app settings. Upon request, your data will be permanently erased from our active databases.

5. Security
We use industry-standard encryption (including HTTPS/SSL) to protect your data in transit and at rest.
